Our Story

Young Stars Family Salon & Spa began in 1999 with a simple aim: dependable beauty and grooming for families in Thane West. The salon has since looked after more than 5000 clients.

Founder Mr. Jayendra G Bhandary brings more than 35 years in the trade. The salon sits opposite HDFC Bank, near Vasant Vihar, on Pokharan Road No. 2.

Clients come from Vasant Vihar, Hiranandani Meadows, Pokharan Road, Majiwada, Ghodbunder Road, and around Upvan Lake.
